## About us | 关于我们

**English**

Carbonstop (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd., founded in 2011, is a carbon management software and consulting solutions provider. We help companies and organizations measure, manage, reduce, and disclose greenhouse gas emissions, supporting their transition toward carbon neutrality.

Our services span integrated carbon management software, carbon accounting and consulting, product carbon footprints, supply-chain carbon management, carbon data, and related professional services. Carbonstop has served more than 1,500 companies and institutions, including over 150 Fortune Global 500 companies and internationally recognized organizations.

**中文**

碳阻迹（北京）科技有限公司成立于 2011 年，是专业的碳管理软件及咨询解决方案提供商。我们帮助企业和机构开展温室气体排放核算、管理、减排与披露，推动各行业迈向碳中和。

我们的服务覆盖一体化碳管理软件、碳核算与咨询、产品碳足迹、供应链碳管理、碳数据及相关专业服务。碳阻迹已服务 1,500+ 家企业机构，其中包括 150+ 家世界 500 强公司及国际知名机构。

## What we build | 核心能力

### Ccloud | 碳云

An integrated carbon management platform for corporate carbon accounting, product carbon footprints, supply-chain carbon management, carbon disclosure, and decarbonization workflows.

面向企业碳核算、产品碳足迹、供应链碳管理、碳披露与减排工作的数字化一体化碳管理平台。

### CCDB | 碳排放因子数据库

Professional carbon emission factor data and tools that support factor search, carbon accounting, product carbon footprints, and sustainability applications.

为排放因子查询、碳排放核算、产品碳足迹及可持续发展应用提供专业的数据与工具支持。

### AI & Open Source | AI 与开源

We are bringing professional carbon data capabilities to AI agents through open standards such as the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and reusable Agent Skills.

我们通过模型上下文协议（MCP）与可复用的 Agent Skills，将专业碳数据能力开放给 AI Agent 与开发者生态。
